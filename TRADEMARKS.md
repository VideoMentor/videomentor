# VideoMentor name and logo

The code is open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0. The **VideoMentor** name and logo are not part of that license: they identify the official project, so people know what they're getting.

## You may
- Say that your project is based on, works with, or is a fork of VideoMentor.
- Use the name and logo, unchanged, to refer to the official project (in articles, talks, tutorials or links).
- Run the official, unchanged VideoMentor for yourself or your organization.

## Please ask first
- Before selling or offering a hosted service under the VideoMentor name or logo.
- Before using the name or logo for an event, a course or merchandise.

## You may not
- Name a changed version, a fork or a product "VideoMentor", or use a confusingly similar name or logo.
- Suggest that your version or service is official or endorsed when it isn't.

Forks are welcome: give yours its own name and logo.

Questions: open an issue on the project.
